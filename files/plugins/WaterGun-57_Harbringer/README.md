Adds **1 new moon — Harbringer**. A vast, frozen ocean moon shrouded in myth. Ancient legends speak of colossal sea creatures that once roamed the depths — powerful enough to destroy the main bridge, which has since been rebuilt. A boat remains on the moon, said to sail toward terrible places.
