## Moon: **[ GRANDUCTOR ]**

A stormy mountain moon that takes place within an unmarked lightning farm, one so large it spans every inch of the moon's surface. Despite its lack of human inhabitants and seemingly empty bunkers and facilities, Granductor is not abandoned. After over a century of work, the moon has fully-automated maintenance, and its endless storms supply an incredible amount of energy to the entire Thistle Nebulae... Or at least, that's what it was supposed to be, before a horrible tectonic event shattered the moon's surface.

## [ FEATURES ]


* *Endless Storms* - The moon is pelted by constant rain and lightning, making even the safest of steps dangerous. Be very careful.

[![image.png](https://i.postimg.cc/m2gt2fWP/image.png)](https://postimg.cc/qgWkjFRr)

* *Lightning Collectors* - Enormous lightning rods cover the surface of the moon, attracting lightning towards them constantly to provide power. Be careful when flying around with jetpacks! 

[![Screenshot-2026-09-01-223108.png](https://i.postimg.cc/4y572Xkk/Screenshot-2026-09-01-223108.png)](https://postimg.cc/648pyJpY)

* *Iron Bulls* - To keep them from escaping the bunkers, coil-heads were plastered with metal scraps and conductive materials, blinded and deafened by nails.
