## SimpleCompany

### After nearly 2 years I have returned to update this mod with overhauled textures, models, and a new cosmetic

Adds 21 cosmetics for MoreCompany all themed around tools/things the employees could use. More may be added eventually.

Feedback/Issues? Please DM me on Discord `Smxrez`.
