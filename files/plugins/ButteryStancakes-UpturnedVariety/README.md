# Upturned Variety
Adds the [boombox music from The Upturned](https://youtu.be/tI40uH2QJiE) as an additional track on the Boombox selection, as well as adding some alternate item skins:
- Gift box (purple & blue)
- Candy (red w/ yellow marbling, pink, chocolate bar)
- Perfume bottle (red, blue, black; +5 bottle shapes)
- Pill bottle (red-orange)
- Mug (blue steel)
- Control pad (green)
- Plastic fish (yellow & red)
- Steering wheel (black plastic)
- Jar of pickles (candy jar)
- Fancy lamp (simple pastels)
- Boombox (shiny silver)
- Cash register (toy)
- Golden cup (glass)
- Plastic cup (styrofoam)

Now also includes a bonus track for the record player.

This mod is 100% client-side, but the boombox track selection will (harmlessly) desync between players, unless everyone has it installed.
