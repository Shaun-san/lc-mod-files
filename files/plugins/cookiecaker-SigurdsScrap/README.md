# Sigurd's Scrap

A scrap mod for all the items Sigurd mentions in various logs that are not able to be found in the vanilla game. Search for them no longer! 

All items are configurable and can be enabled/disabled by their respective bundles.

## Contents
- **Frying pan:** *Mummy - Aug 24*
- **Big nail:** *Mummy - Aug 24*
- **Scissors (pair of scissors):** *Goodbye - Sep 7*
- **Box of stamps (box full of stamps):** *Goodbye - Sep 7*
- **Bundle of cords:** *Goodbye - Sep 7*
- **Metal grinder (rusty metal grinder):** *Work - Oct 15*
